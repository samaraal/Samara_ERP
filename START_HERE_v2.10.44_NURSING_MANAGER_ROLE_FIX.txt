SAMARA ERP v2.10.44 — NURSING MANAGER ROLE FIX

What the screenshot revealed
----------------------------
The Nursing Manager login is showing ERP role "Manager".
v2.10.43 wrongly required:
ERP Role = Nurse
AND Designation = Nursing Manager

v2.10.44 fixes this.

New rule
--------
If Designation = Nursing Manager, My Quick Tasks + Voice Entry are available
regardless of whether ERP access role is Nurse or Manager.

This keeps access tied to the actual staff Designation, not the ERP permission role.
