- Copy the complete code from:
  supabase/functions/staff-translate-tamil/index.ts
- Deploy the function.
- Existing GEMINI_API_KEY and OPENAI_API_KEY secrets are reused. No new key is required.

3. REPLACE ON GITHUB
- app.js
- styles.css
- index.html
- service-worker.js

HOW IT WORKS
- English remains the official clinical record.
- Staff tap "தமிழில் பார்க்க" only when Tamil assistance is required.
- Gemini translates first; OpenAI is the automatic fallback.
- The Tamil translation is cached and reused instead of repeatedly spending AI quota.
- Staff can tap "தமிழில் கேட்க" to hear it or "தமிழை மறைக்க" to close it.
- Medicine names, doses, routes, measurements, dates and times are instructed to remain unchanged.

- General Handover priority worklist.
- Patient special instructions and precautions.
- Care-plan instructions and nursing remarks.
- Incident description, immediate action and follow-up/closure instruction.
- Medication special instructions.
- Physiotherapy precautions and notes.
