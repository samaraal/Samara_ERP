SAMARA CARE ERP v2.10.19 — DIRECTOR'S OFFICE / TRANSFERABLE STD

This revision does NOT hard-code Akshai, Chellaboomi, or any future employee.

Director's Office now contains two transferable position assignments:
1. Director
2. Secretary to the Director (STD)

The Director assignment does not change the holder's ERP role.
The STD assignment preserves the person's existing Login ID/password and automatically:
- stores their old ERP role, department and designation
- changes access role to STD
- moves employment display to Director's Office
- sets designation to Secretary to the Director (STD)

When the STD is changed later:
- the previous STD's old role/department/designation are restored automatically
- the new holder receives STD access
- no portal code change is needed

DEPLOYMENT
1. Supabase SQL Editor: run 104_directors_office_position_assignment.sql once.
2. Supabase Edge Functions > admin-users: replace index.ts and deploy.
3. Website root: replace app.js, index.html and service-worker.js.
4. Open ERP > App Help > Repair App once if an old version remains cached.
5. HR Dashboard > Director's Office:
   - Assign Director = Chellaboomi
   - Assign STD = Akshai

Important: selecting Akshai does not create a duplicate employee and does not change her Login ID/password.
Future STD changes are made only from HR > Director's Office > Change Assignment.
