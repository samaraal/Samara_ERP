SAMARA CARE ERP v2.10.21 — STD ELEGANT COMMUNICATION DASHBOARD

CHANGES
-------
1. Clinical alerts are now disabled at the SOURCE for STD.
   The STD clinical engine does not call the clinical-alert RPC, does not start
   its one-minute timer, removes any existing clinical overlay and cancels any
   clinical speech. This fixes the large CLINICAL ALERT banner shown in the
   Director's Office screenshot.

2. Director's Office dashboard improved:
   - cleaner executive-style communication cards
   - WhatsApp Enquiries
   - Feedback
   - existing Appointments, Calls, Follow-ups, Visitors, Correspondence, Reminders

3. STD access now includes:
   - Director's Office
   - WhatsApp Inbox
   - Feedback
   - My Leave & Permission

4. STD remains transferable. Nothing is hard-coded to Akshai.

INSTALL
-------
A. Supabase SQL Editor:
   Run 106_std_whatsapp_feedback_access.sql once.

B. ERP hosting:
   Replace:
   - app.js
   - index.html
   - service-worker.js

C. Ctrl+F5 once after upload.

DO NOT rerun SQL 104 or 105.
DO NOT change WhatsApp Edge Functions.
DO NOT change the Director/Admin role.
