SAMARA ERP v2.10.40 — DIRECTOR'S OFFICE iPHONE LAYOUT FIX

Problem
-------
On iPhone the Director's Office dashboard was wider than the screen.
The Quick Task / New Item area and right-hand dashboard cards could extend
outside the visible viewport.

Fix
---
- Director's Office header now stays within the phone width.
- Quick Task and New Item buttons resize/wrap safely.
- Dashboard statistics use two true flexible columns on mobile.
- Communication cards use one full-width column on mobile.
- Long text wraps instead of forcing horizontal page width.
- No change to Voice Entry or OpenAI processing.

INSTALL

1. app.js
2. index.html
3. service-worker.js

NO SQL.
