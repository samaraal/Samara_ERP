SAMARA CARE ERP 2.11.97 — MOBILE HORIZONTAL-SCROLL AUDIT

AUDIT RESULT
The application source contains 32 rendered table definitions, in addition to several custom grids and tab strips. Standard tables inherited desktop minimum widths of approximately 680–1120 pixels, which caused right-side scrolling on phones.

AREAS CORRECTED
1. Clinical Alerts and Clinical Escalation registers
2. Notifications — Pharmacy & Stores requests and overdue clinical items
3. HR — Career Applications and Interviews
4. HR — unlinked/legacy login accounts and Nursing staff lists
5. Admission Enquiries
6. Discharge workflow and financial-clearance registers
7. Vital Signs history and patient trends
8. Shift-task and clinical activity registers
9. Food & Beverage records
10. Patient Documents register
11. Patient Medication history, modification/review and MAR tables
12. Intelligent Report clinical annexure tables
13. Accounts reports and Patient Ledger tables
14. Stores assignment, receipts, stock, indent and handover registers
15. Chargeable Items / Charge Master tables
16. WhatsApp delivery/report logs
17. Recovery Timeline
18. Audit/history and other standard ERP data tables
19. General filter tabs, time chips, patient badges and mail folder controls

MOBILE BEHAVIOUR
- Wide rows become compact two-column labelled cards.
- Long content and Action fields use the full card width.
- Buttons appear inside each card and do not require rightward scrolling.
- Empty-state rows remain full width.
- Tabs/chips wrap within the phone width instead of scrolling sideways.
- Inputs, selects, modals and main content are constrained to the viewport.

PRESERVED CUSTOM MOBILE LAYOUTS
- Rooms compact cards
- Patient Master compact list
- Employee Master compact list
- Medication action/log cards

DESKTOP / WINDOWS
Desktop tables and their column layouts remain unchanged.
