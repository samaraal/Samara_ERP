SAMARA CARE ERP v2.8.95 — CLINICAL ALERT REGULARISATION

RULE IMPLEMENTED
1. Everything pending UP TO installation time is consolidated into ONE alert:
   "One-time Clinical Backlog Regularisation".
2. Historical pending medication/vitals/care/physio items do NOT generate separate retroactive alerts.
3. Click "Regularise Backlog" once to acknowledge that historical consolidated alert.
4. After the cutoff, alerts operate normally item-by-item.
5. Future unresolved alerts escalate to Manager/Admin after the configured manager escalation interval (default 30 minutes).
6. Medication Dashboard counts/worklist are NOT changed by this update.

INSTALLATION
A. First run in Supabase SQL Editor:
   109_CLINICAL_ALERT_REGULARISATION_ONE_BACKLOG_THEN_NORMAL.sql
B. Then upload/replace only:
   app.js
   index.html
   service-worker.js

NO styles.css. NO folders.

After SQL, the result will show the one-time backlog counts captured at the cutoff.
