SAMARA ERP v2.10.47 — EMPLOYMENT & SALARY HISTORY

Adds Employment & Salary History to Employee Personnel File.

Actions: Promotion, Transfer, Designation Change, Department Change, Reporting Change,
Annual Increment, Salary Revision, Confirmation, Demotion, Suspension, Reinstatement.

Salary: current/new salary, salary frequency, annual increment amount and percentage.
Each action preserves previous department, designation, ERP role and salary.

Promotion/designation and ERP Access Role remain separate.

INSTALL:
1. Run 116_employee_employment_salary_history.sql once. SQL 115 must already be installed.
2. Replace app.js, index.html, service-worker.js.
3. Use App Help -> Repair App if mobile cache persists.

No Edge Function/API key changes.
