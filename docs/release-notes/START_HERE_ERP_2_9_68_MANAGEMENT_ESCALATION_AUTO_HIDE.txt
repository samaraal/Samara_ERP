SAMARA CARE ERP v2.9.68 — MANAGEMENT ESCALATION AUTO-HIDE

CHANGE
- Administrator / Manager Clinical Escalation popup displays for approximately 8 seconds.
- It then auto-hides so normal ERP work can continue.
- If still unresolved, it can appear again after the configured alert repeat interval (normally 5 minutes).
- The escalation is NOT acknowledged or resolved by auto-hide.
- It remains continuously available in the Clinical Alert bell and Clinical Escalations register.
- Nurse / Caregiver clinical-task popup behaviour is unchanged and remains persistent.
- Existing “Open to Resolve” and “Remind in 5 min” controls remain available while the popup is visible.

3. service-worker.js
