SAMARA CARE ERP v2.8.96 — CLINICAL ESCALATIONS DASHBOARD

Purpose:
- Adds a dedicated Clinical Escalations page for Manager and Administrator.
- Adds an Open Clinical Escalations card to the Admin/Manager home dashboard.
- Shows type, patient, room/bed, priority, escalation time, elapsed time and reason.
- Manager/Admin can open the source area and mark an escalation resolved.
- Resolution remarks are mandatory and stored in the database.
- The one-time historical backlog remains ONE Regularisation escalation.
- Future alerts continue to escalate normally after the configured threshold.

INSTALL:
1. Run 110_CLINICAL_ESCALATION_DASHBOARD_MANAGER_ADMIN.sql in Supabase SQL Editor.
2. Replace only app.js, index.html, service-worker.js.
3. No styles.css and no folders.

No medication-count, patient, billing, HR, admission or other unrelated workflow is changed.
