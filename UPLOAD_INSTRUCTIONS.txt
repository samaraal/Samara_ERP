1. The mobile bottom navigation automatically hides while an entry pop-up/modal is open.

7. Existing Daily Care, Physiotherapy, Handover, Incident and other Samara toasts inherit the
   standard green-success / red-failure presentation.

No SQL / Supabase change is required.
