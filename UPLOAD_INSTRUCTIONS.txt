SAMARA CARE ERP v2.8.29 — BLOOD GROUP DROPDOWN

Change:
Blood Group is now a controlled dropdown for BOTH:
- Patients
- Employees

Allowed values:
A+
A-
B+
B-
AB+
AB-
O+
O-
Unknown

Purpose:
- Prevent typing mistakes such as O Positive, O+, 0+, AB Positive, etc.
- Keep patient and employee records standardised for reports and clinical use.

No SQL / Supabase change is required because the existing blood_group column remains text.

Upload/replace:
- app.js
- index.html
- service-worker.js

Version remains v2.8.29.
