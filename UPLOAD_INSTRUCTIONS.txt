SAMARA CARE ERP v2.8.26 — CONFIRMED SAVE FEEDBACK

This update fixes the confirmation issue at the mechanism level.

Why the previous notification could disappear:
- Several modules refreshed data or navigated immediately after saving.
- React component notifications could therefore be unmounted before staff saw them.

What v2.8.26 changes:
1. The save/failure confirmation is appended directly to document.body.
2. Its visibility/layout uses INLINE styling — it does not depend on CSS loading or cache.
3. It uses the maximum practical z-index, above ERP modals and mobile navigation.
4. Success is GREEN with a large ✓.
5. Failure is RED with a large !.
6. It stays for 10 seconds or until staff presses OK.
7. Mobile vibration feedback is used when supported.
8. Medication and Vital Signs confirmation occurs immediately after the database save,
   BEFORE any reload or navigation.
9. Existing local showToast/notify functions are also connected to the global overlay.

Patched local showToast helpers: 6
Patched notify helpers: 1

- app.js
- index.html
- service-worker.js

NO SQL / SUPABASE CHANGE.
