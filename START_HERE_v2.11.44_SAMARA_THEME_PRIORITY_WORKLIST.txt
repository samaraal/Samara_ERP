HOW THE PRIORITY WORKLIST IS CREATED
------------------------------------
The Priority Worklist is automatic. Nurses do not type a separate worklist.

1. MEDICINES
An active medication becomes visible when its scheduled time is reached and no
administration status has been recorded. It progresses through Due Now,
Overdue, Escalation Due and Critical according to the configured time limits.

2. VITAL SIGNS
Every active patient appears until at least one Vital Signs record is entered
for that patient today.

3. CURRENT-SHIFT CARE
Care activities in the patient's Master Care Plan appear when assigned to the
current Day or Night shift. They disappear when completed through Daily Care.

4. DISCHARGE
A discharge appears for final nursing action after Accounts clearance.

SIMPLE TEST
-----------
Select an active patient whose Master Care Plan contains an activity assigned
to the current shift. Do not complete it in Daily Care. Open Nursing Dashboard
and press Refresh. The patient and care activity will appear in Priority
Worklist. Complete it through Shift Tasks / Daily Care and it will disappear.
